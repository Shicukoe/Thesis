  Build a simple ERP web application for a small business with a clean foundation that can be gradually expanded. Users should be able to create, edit, delete, and browse customers. Add products. Each product should have a name, SKU, price, and current
  stock quantity. A top navigation bar with Dashboard, Customers, Vendors, and Products should be enough for now. The dashboard doesn't need much yet. Just show the total number of customers and products. Implement sales orders. A sales order should
  belong to one customer and contain one or more products with quantities. The total amount should be calculated automatically from the selected products and quantities. Don't let users confirm the order if any item doesn't have enough stock available.
  When a sales order is confirmed, reduce the inventory automatically. Whenever a sales order is confirmed, automatically generate an invoice. Invoices should have a due date 15 days after they're created. Invoices also need a payment status. Use these
  three statuses: Unpaid, Partially Paid, and Paid. Users should be able to record payments against invoices, supporting Cash and Bank Transfer as payment methods. Implement purchase orders so staff can restock inventory. When a purchase order is marked
  as received, increase the product stock automatically. Add vendor management. Users should be able to create, edit, delete, and browse vendors. Instead of permanently deleting vendors, move them to a recycle bin where they can be viewed and restored.
  Introduce Admin, Manager, and Staff user roles. Managers and Admins can delete customers and products, but Staff cannot. Customers and products use permanent deletion based on the user's permissions, and do not use a recycle bin. Managers and Staff
  are not allowed to manage user accounts (the User Accounts management view is restricted to Admin only). Each product has its own configurable low-stock threshold, and products are shown as low stock on the dashboard when their stock drops below this
  threshold. Add a monthly sales report view showing the total sales for each month in a table, with filters for a custom date range. Staff members should only be able to view report data for orders that they created themselves, whereas Managers and
  Admins can view report data for all orders. Make the dashboard clean, professional, and visually appealing. Add pagination to all list pages, including customers, products, vendors, sales orders, purchase orders, and invoices. Connect a global search
  bar in the header to quickly locate products and customers by name. Ensure that product prices and stock quantities are never allowed to have negative values. Maintain a clean overall codebase structure with clear comments explaining the main business
  logic. Provide a monthly sales report export feature that generates and downloads an Excel (.xlsx) file. Add a business event audit log that tracks whenever a sales order is confirmed, an invoice is generated, or a payment is recorded, displaying the
  exact date and time, the specific user who performed the action, their active user role, event type, and details. Provide a user interface to browse these logs.
